## Verdict against `v4.0.0`

Attacking from a cell with no water path to the piranha leaves it unable to see or reach you. Lightning arcs 2 tiles from targets in water and deals full damage to all when the main target is in water. The Huntress's current kit is the Spirit Bow (the boomerang is a generic thrown weapon, not her starter); propping the door is irrelevant because piranhas never leave water.
