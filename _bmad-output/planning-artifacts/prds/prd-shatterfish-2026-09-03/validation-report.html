high

Composite outcome contradicts SM-C2

§ 3 Glossary "Composite outcome"; § 7 SM-C2
The Glossary orders the per-Run result "win, then depth reached, then turns survived", so among losses a deeper, faster death ranks above a shallower, longer one. SM-C2 says "A Brain that dives and dies deeper is worse" and offers "the Composite outcome and the survival curve" as the corrective, but the Composite outcome is the thing that rewards diving. The Sequential test (FR-21) optimizes this ordering, so the Rig will accept Brains that dive.
Fix: decide the ordering on purpose and say what was given up. Options: (a) keep depth-first and reword SM-C2 to "depth reached alone, outside the Composite outcome"; (b) rank losses by a survival-weighted measure (depth with a turns floor, or the boss staircase) and note the cost in test power; (c) make the ordering a pre-registered parameter of the Hypothesis ID. Record the choice in the E3 statistics ADR.
Status: fixed in v1

FR-2 does not say whether a process may run more than one Run

§ 4.1 FR-2; § 4.4 FR-19; § 6.2; § 8 NFR-3
FR-19 says "One process per Run by default", NFR-3 and FR-5 measure "Runs per minute for one process", and section 6.2 calls classloader isolation "a spike in E1, not a requirement; processes are the default". The Composite outcome rewards dive-and-die deeper in the pre-win era that SM-C2 says it prevents

§3 Composite outcome, FR-21, SM-C2

  • Rule 4 process boundary: §2.1 "in one JVM", §5 "not ... a separate process or over a socket", §10 Runtime "the bot in the same process as the game". Rig parallelism uses whole game-plus-Brain processes, not a bot process. No socket, RPC, or IPC appears anywhere.
  • Rule 6 native UI: FR-38's consequence puts the absence of Swing, JavaFX, ImGui, and web views on the classpath under test; §5 restates it. No FR asks for a toolkit the game does not have.
  • Rule 7: FR-52 and FR-53 cover ADRs, the ideas ledger, docs-with-code, TODO-with-issue, and issue mirroring.
  • Rule 3 upstream/master: FR-50, NFR-5, and §5 all forbid it; upgrades are tag-only and the procedure is enumerated.
  • Oracle mode: off by default, explicit flag, marked in the Panel, refused by the Rig for ranked comparisons, recorded on every Results page, and not exposable through the Rig command line (FR-11, FR-43, §9, addendum). The only gap found is the unmarked Run log (A, low).
  • Module edges: api depends on nothing; brain on api only; ArchUnit and a resolution-time check both enforce (FR-7, §10). The overlay module depending on core is consistent with Rule 6 and does not touch the Brain's edges.
  • Thread confinement: FR-12 and NFR-4 agree (Brain on a worker thread over an immutable Observation, Panel writes on the render thread).
  • Network and telemetry: NFR-8 forbids both; no FR needs a network call.
  • Determinism test and fairness suite in CI on every pull request: FR-2, FR-8, NFR-1, SM-8 agree.
