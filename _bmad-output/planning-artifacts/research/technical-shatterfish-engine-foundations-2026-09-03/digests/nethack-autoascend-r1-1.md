### (b) AutoAscend
- CLAIM: AutoAscend is a Python bot over NLE, first place in the 2021 challenge; repository github.com/maciej-sypetkowski/autoascend (65 stars / 15 forks at fetch time), with a talk overview at a linked video timestamp 1:14:00-1:21:21. | SOURCE: https://github.com/maciej-sypetkowski/autoascend | PUBLISHER: Maciej Sypetkowski (GitHub) | PUB_DATE: unknown (repo circa 2021-2022) | CONFIDENCE: high | CLASS: ecosystem
- CLAIM: Architecture is a strategy framework: `strategy.py` defines a base Strategy = "entering condition and agent's behavior"; strategies compose via a functional interface (`repeat`, `until`, `preempt`) or run imperatively inside other strategies. | SOURCE: https://github.com/maciej-sypetkowski/autoascend | PUBLISHER: AutoAscend README | PUB_DATE: unknown | CONFIDENCE: high | CLASS: pattern
- CLAIM: Module layout: `global_logic.py` holds the main strategy and high-level strategies (altar farming, altar item identification, dipping for Excalibur); `agent.py` wraps NLE actions into atomic and complex actions; separate modules for inventory/item identification/equipment selection, `combat/`, `exploration_logic.py`, and a `soko_solver` for Sokoban. | SOURCE: https://github.com/maciej-sypetkowski/autoascend | PUBLISHER: AutoAscend README | PUB_DATE: unknown | CONFIDENCE: high | CLASS: pattern
- CLAIM: Item beliefs are kept by an item manager holding "known glyph to object and object to glyph mapping", i.e. an explicit identification belief table, not a learned model. | SOURCE: https://github.com/maciej-sypetkowski/autoascend | PUBLISHER: AutoAscend README | PUB_DATE: unknown | CONFIDENCE: medium | CLASS: pattern
- CLAIM: AutoAscend is described as eleven goal-directed high-level strategies organized as a "directed acyclic graph of explicitly defined strategies" with "a complex predicate based control flow scheme to determine which strategy to query for an action"; example strategies: fighting arbitrary monsters, selecting safe food, efficient exploration while gathering and identifying items. | SOURCE: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2305.19240 | PUBLISHER: Piterbarg et al., NeurIPS 2023 | PUB_DATE: 2023-05 | CONFIDENCE: high | CLASS: pattern

### (e) Lessons for symbolic bot builders
- CLAIM: What made AutoAscend work per the challenge report and follow-up: explicit hierarchy (strategy DAG with entry predicates), domain-knowledge-heavy subroutines, hand-written observation parsers, and explicit belief tables for item identification -- exactly the components neural entries lacked. | SOURCE: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2203.11889 ; https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2305.19240 | PUBLISHER: arXiv | PUB_DATE: 2022-03 / 2023-05 | CONFIDENCE: high | CLASS: pattern
- CLAIM: The most successful neural entry (RAPH) was itself a hybrid that kept a hard-coded hierarchical skill framework and only learned combat -- evidence that a learned component slots in late, at the tactical leaf, under symbolic arbitration. | SOURCE: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2203.11889 | PUBLISHER: arXiv 2203.11889 | PUB_DATE: 2022-03 | CONFIDENCE: medium | CLASS: pattern
- CLAIM: Failure modes: AutoAscend's median ~5k is "only just above a Beginner score"; mean dungeon level ~3 and ~19.6k turns mean it dies early relative to the 50-level game; the bot is brittle to inputs outside its own action stream. | SOURCE: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2203.11889 ; https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2305.19240 ; https://github.com/maciej-sypetkowski/autoascend | PUBLISHER: arXiv / GitHub | PUB_DATE: 2022-2023 | CONFIDENCE: high | CLASS: failure
- CLAIM: Maintainability patterns across the tradition: config-file tunable priorities (Borg `borg.txt`), goal lists as data (qw), composable strategy combinators (AutoAscend `repeat/until/preempt`), and a goal-loop of "evaluate goals in order, act one step, observe" (Borg). | SOURCE: https://angband.readthedocs.io/en/latest/hacking/borg.html ; https://github.com/crawl/qw ; https://github.com/maciej-sypetkowski/autoascend | PUBLISHER: various | PUB_DATE: unknown | CONFIDENCE: medium | CLASS: pattern

## Contradictions
- AutoAscend median score: challenge report says ~5,300 (competition test seeds, Oct 2021); Piterbarg et al. 2023 reproduction gives median 4,918 / mean 8,556. Differences plausibly from seeds, NLE version, and episode count; treat "median ~5k" as the robust figure.
- Symbolic-vs-neural median gap: challenge report "nearly threefold"; Piterbarg et al. say "over four times". Depends on which neural entry is counted (RAPH hybrid vs pure-neural KakaoBrain).
- Jan 2026 blog quotes a "2015 BotHack-based agent" ascension; the BotHack YAAP thread is the primary source and I could not fetch it to confirm the date and non-wizard-mode claim directly.
