## Contradictions
- ISMCTS-vs-PIMC dominance is domain-dependent, not settled: the York papers (2011, 2012) report ISMCTS winning on LOTR: Confrontation and Phantom games, while the same literature summarised in the CIG 2011/thesis line reports PIMC ahead in highly stochastic multi-action domains. Treat "ISMCTS beats determinization" as conditional on tree depth and strategy-fusion severity (Long et al. 2010 gives the predictive properties).
- Long et al. 2010 argue PIMC's theoretical flaws often do not matter in practice; Frank and Basin 1998 and the 2024 EPIMC paper argue strategy fusion is worth an algorithmic fix. Both are consistent if the game has low disambiguation and high leaf correlation, which should be measured for Shattered Pixel Dungeon rather than assumed.

## Leads
- Whitehouse 2014 PhD thesis (https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/8117/) contains the fullest practical treatment of determinization counts, iteration budgets, and inference/opponent modelling in ISMCTS; full-text PDFs of the 1998, 2010 and 2012 papers could not be parsed this run and should be read directly for numbers.
- Long et al. 2010 full PDF (https://webdocs.cs.ualberta.ca/~nathanst/papers/pimc.pdf) for the three named properties and the three strategy-fusion-safe conditions; compute the disambiguation factor for SPD (how fast unidentified items/traps resolve) to decide whether plain redetermination suffices.
- POMCP successors (DESPOT, POMCPOW) and the JuliaPOMDP POMCP.jl implementation (https://juliapomdp.github.io/POMCP.jl/latest/) for single-agent belief-state MCTS engineering details.
- "Sound Algorithms in Imperfect Information Games" (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.08740) for a formal statement of why PIMC/ISMCTS are unsound and what soundness would require.
- Campbell and Verbrugge, "Learning Combat in NetHack" (AIIDE 2017, https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/12923/12923-52-16440-1-2-20201228.pdf) for a hand-built abstract combat model of a roguelike.
- Monte Carlo search algorithm discovery for one-player games (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.4692) for single-player MCTS variants (nested MC) relevant to horizon budgeting.
