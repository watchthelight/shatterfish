## Contradictions
- The brief presumes forks add docked panels in Noosa; the one fork found with a "desktop overlay" (DaltonGoesFast QoL) actually does its big HUD as an external Godot companion app + Python/HTTP gateway, only adding small Noosa widgets in-game. No fork with a large in-engine docked debug panel was located this run (absence of evidence, not evidence of impossibility).
- Brief question (d) assumes an `addKeyBinding`-style registration API; KeyBindings.java has none. Registration is by adding to SPDAction's static `defaultBindings` map and calling `setAllBindings`.
