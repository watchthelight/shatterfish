## Contradictions
- Seed reproducibility: Evan's blog says a seed makes generation "always give the same result", but shpd-toolkit warns identical seeds are only "likely, but not guaranteed" to produce the same levels across patch versions, and the Lemmy guide notes seeds break across versions. Resolution: determinism holds per-build, not across versions -- a pinned fork must freeze the exact upstream tag.
- Coop-Shattered-Pixel-Dungeon-gtx is indexed as an "RL bot" but the page shows no RL methodology or results; treat the RL claim as self-described only.
