## Contradictions
- Round-1 summary calls AutoAscend's arbitration a "predicate DAG"; the team's own text calls it "a custom variation of a behaviour tree capable of dynamic tree expansion, including recursions", and the code is a linear preemption list over generator strategies (strategy.py / global_logic.py). Both fit; "DAG" is the NetHack-is-Hard-to-Hack paper's framing, not the authors'. Terminology, not a factual conflict.
- fight_heur.py considers only visible monsters, while the report says the parser keeps "track of monsters including peaceful monsters"; consistent once you note that tracking is one-frame (peaceful classification) rather than persistent off-screen belief.
- The neural-track leaderboard reports raph median 1727.5 but mean 1350.81 (mean < median), consistent with the report's note that Neural 1st shows "evidence of early episode termination".
