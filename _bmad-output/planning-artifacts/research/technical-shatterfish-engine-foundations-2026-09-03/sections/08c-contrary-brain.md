### Contrary evidence: the brain architecture

A fresh-context skeptic attacked the symbolic-first recommendation. One headline finding was overturned on re-reading the source, three survive in weakened form, and one is a genuine caution:

- **Overturned.** The skeptic reported LLM agents reaching 40% NetHack progression on BALROG in February 2026. The leaderboard's LLM table has the columns "MiniHack | NetHack | Date"; the Gemini-3-Pro row reads MiniHack 40.0 ± 7.7, NetHack 6.8 ± 3.2 [28]. Section 5's figure stands. What survives is the slope: from about 1.6% in late 2024 to 6.8% in early 2026 with no game-specific engineering [120, 28], so "learned components are late" is a scheduling choice to revisit annually, not a law.
- **The symbolic champion is plateaued.** No agent of any architecture has ascended NetHack 3.6.6 or later; the one recorded bot ascension used an exploit since removed [121]. AutoAscend's rule network was opaque enough that a research team had to instrument it to recover its strategy labels [122], and the behaviour-tree literature warns that such hierarchies become "intractable" as behaviours multiply and are static in unanticipated situations [123, 124]. Shatterfish inherits this cost; mitigations are playbooks as data, a strategy log the overlay can show, and per-epic retrospectives, none of which AutoAscend had.
- **Privileged input.** AutoAscend consumed the NetHack Learning Environment's structured glyph and stats feed, a privileged semantic interface, whereas Shatterfish's rule restricts the bot to what a human could see [125]. This is a fair point about cost, not feasibility: the Observer builds a semantic observation from what the renderer draws, which is closer to NLE's feed than to pixels, but the perception and belief layer is work AutoAscend never did and E1 must budget it.
- **Determinization is the pattern ISMCTS replaced, and the "high disambiguation" claim is unmeasured.** Both points are already in section 4: Long et al. require the properties to be detected in the real game, and the lead's roguelike inference is marked low confidence; item identification is explicitly named there as the poker-like regime that belongs to belief reasoning rather than search. The skeptic strengthens the requirement that E6 measure before choosing.

Not surviving: any evidence that a learned or hybrid agent has beaten the symbolic bots on a roguelike (the skeptic searched and found none), or that a priority-list design was abandoned by anyone who built one.
