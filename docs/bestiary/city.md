- **Accuracy vs evasion roll.** A hit lands when Random.Float(attackSkill) \* modifiers &gt;= Random.Float(defenseSkill) \* modifiers. Magic attacks call hit(...,magic=true), which doubles the attacker's accuracy roll (accMulti 2).
