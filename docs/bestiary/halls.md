### Mechanics

Tier 1: read from the code at the pinned tag.

- **Movement.** baseSpeed 1, but blinks (teleports) toward its hunt target cell when that cell is in its FOV and more than 2 tiles away. Net time cost of the blink is zero: getCloser spends -1/speed and Hunting then spends +1/speed, so it can blink and attack in the same turn. The blink cooldown is set to 4-6, and it only counts down on getCloser calls that walk instead of blinking; it does not tick while the succubus stands and attacks. Speed `normal`; flies: no; amphibious: no; water-bound: no; opens doors: yes; follows you on the stairs: no. <small>`core/…/mobs/Succubus.java:111`, `core/…/mobs/Succubus.java:112`, `core/…/mobs/Succubus.java:115`, `core/…/mobs/Succubus.java:158`, `core/…/mobs/Mob.java:1355`, `core/…/levels/Terrain.java:90`, `core/…/mobs/Mob.java:1686`, `core/…/mobs/Mob.java:1699`, `core/…/mobs/Succubus.java:123`, `core/…/mobs/Succubus.java:151`</small>
- **Attack.** `melee`, reach 1. Adjacent melee (default canAttack).
