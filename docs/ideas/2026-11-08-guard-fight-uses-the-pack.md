# The Guard fight uses the pack

Of the 24 heroes a Prison Guard killed in the story 10.43 Runs, most died holding a known potion of strength, a wand of
disintegration or magic missile, liquid flame or frost, or a stone of fear. The Brain throws only missile weapons and
zaps only the Mage's staff. A Policy that, against an awake Guard 3 to 8 cells away that the hero will not outfight,
zaps a known attack wand and throws a known liquid flame or frost potion at it (clear of grass the fire could carry
back) would use what the hero already holds. Needs: the burn and freeze numbers at the pinned tag with `path:line`, the
grass check from the drawn tiles, and a `fairness` review. Measure on the standard set at depth 7 and 8 deaths to Guards.
