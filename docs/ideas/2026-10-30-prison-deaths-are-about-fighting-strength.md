# Aim the next Prison work at fighting strength, not food

Story 10.39 (the Prison food, second pass) measured five levers on the food and rest side and found none that adds
reach: less rest before the descent (A, B) cut the share of heroes starving on their last Decision by 12 and 15 points
and lost reach and Goo kills; more rest (E) lost reach and Goo kills and left the share where it was. Rest is near a
local optimum on this Brain, and the starving share is the price of surviving, not the cause of dying. The Prison
deaths are about fighting strength.

What the next work could look at, in the order the evidence points:

- Story 10.17 (the power curve by depth): the hero's level, strength, armour and weapon at arrival on each depth,
  against the depth's killers. `arrivals.tsv` of the 10.7 diagnosis already carries level and strength at arrival; gear
  is the missing column.
- Combat: which killer ends the Runs that arrive healthy and fed, and whether the fight Policy had a choice (a retreat, a
  door, a throw) that the Run log shows it did not take.
- What the 10.39 page cannot show: a lever that changes who arrives where changes every later fight, so a table of 40
  seeds per class moves by 5 either way on noise. A lever on this side wants the second slice before it is kept.

Not to be re-tried without a new reason: the descent-rest share (`descend.rest_per_mille`, `descend.rest_tight_per_mille`),
a rest before exploring on when hurt, and a smaller search budget (see the 10.39 page for the tables).
