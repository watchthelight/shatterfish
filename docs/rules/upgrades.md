# Rules: Upgrades

What a scroll of upgrade does to a weapon, an armour, a wand and a ring, what it asks of the strength the hero has, and what reading it onto an item whose level or curse is not shown costs. The Playbook's `upgrade` section (story 7.4) decides by these.

Every row was read from the pinned tag `v4.0.2` in story 7.4; links open the cited lines at the tag. Tier 1 means the code confirms the rule.
