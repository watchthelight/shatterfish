## Not confirmed

What the reader looked for and could not settle from the code; each item is an open question for the epic that needs it.

- Which buffs *outside* `actors/buffs/` (talent trackers, item/artifact inner-class buffs, ability buffs) return `NONE`: only 9 files in the whole tree contain `return BuffIndicator.NONE`, but subclasses that simply do not override `icon()` inherit NONE and were not enumerated file by file.
- Whether `WndInfoMob` can be opened for a mob seen only through mind vision: examine requires `visited || mapped` (GameScene.java:1661-1667) and mind-vision cells are marked visited (Dungeon.java:942-954), but `getObjectsAtCell` was not read.
- `Amok` has no `DURATION` constant of its own; durations come from each source (not surveyed).
- `Char.resist` returns 1 unless a resistance matches (Char.java:1306-1318); per-mob resistances that halve buff durations were not surveyed.
- The "Darkness" name in the task: no buff of that name exists in `actors/buffs/`; the DARKNESS challenge sets `viewDistance = 2` (Level.java:157) and `Shadows` (positive, no announce) blanks FOV like Blindness (Level.java:1290) — its own effects beyond that were not read.
- Exact per-particle lifetime of blob emitters (how long a puff lingers after a cell leaves `heroFOV`) lives in `Emitter`/particle factories and was not read.
