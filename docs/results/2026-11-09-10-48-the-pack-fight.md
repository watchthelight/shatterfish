# Story 10.48: the fight uses the pack

Heroes die holding known liquid flame, frost and attack wands. The Brain threw only missile weapons and zapped only the Mage's staff. The new
`Pack` Policy (issue #458) throws a known potion of liquid flame or frost, or zaps a known lightning, disintegration, magic missile or frost
wand, at an awake enemy that the hero will not outfight (four or more turns to kill it, `Fight.favourable` false), that is at range and in a
clear line. The mechanics are cited in `docs/rules/combat.md` and the rule is `pack-use` in `docs/brain-rules.md`.

## Method

Two ALL-40 slices of the `standard` set (40 Runs per class, 240 Runs per slice), salt bases 1000 and 3000, `--jobs 4`, current build `1a970b6d5`
against a baseline built from the merge-base `91c24281c` (main after #457, food is worth the turns it buys). Denominators are 240 per slice and 480
pooled in every row below. No goo, holdout or tengu seed was used.

## Pooled over both slices (480 Runs)

| | baseline 91c24281c | with Pack | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| reach depth 5 | 276 | 278 | +2 |
| Goo kills | 226 | 227 | +1 |

Slice 1000 alone: reach depth 5 143 to 141 (-2), Goo kills 120 to 118 (-2). Slice 3000 alone: reach depth 5 133 to 137 (+4), Goo kills 106 to 109 (+3).

## Per class, both slices added (80 Runs each)

| class | reach d5 | Goo kills |
|---|---|---|
| WARRIOR | 62 to 62 (0) | 51 to 53 (+2) |
| MAGE | 49 to 47 (-2) | 44 to 43 (-1) |
| ROGUE | 37 to 34 (-3) | 23 to 19 (-4) |
| HUNTRESS | 48 to 50 (+2) | 39 to 41 (+2) |
| DUELIST | 51 to 53 (+2) | 46 to 47 (+1) |
| CLERIC | 29 to 32 (+3) | 23 to 24 (+1) |

The worst class is Rogue, 4 worse in Goo kills pooled. In slice 1000 alone the Rogue was 5 worse in both reach depth 5 and Goo kills (21 to 16 and
14 to 9); in slice 3000 it was 2 better in reach and 1 better in Goo kills. No class is 8 or more worse in either slice.

## Verdict

The keep rule holds: pooled reach depth 5 and Goo kills are not worse, and no class is 8 or more worse. The Policy is kept. The effect is small,
about half a Run in a hundred, and well inside the noise of a 480-Run pool (a swing of 3 is routine at this size); the page does not claim a gain.
What it shows is that using the pack does no harm in the table, and that the Policy fires.

## What the table cannot show

- In the current build of slice 1000, 67 of 240 Runs show at least one Pack Decision (59 flame throws, 46 frost throws, 63 disintegration, 38
  magic missile, 16 lightning and 10 frost wand zaps). The baseline shows none. So the lever is used, and the effect on the end result is small
  because the end of a Run depends on much besides these fights.
- The prison guard is the last screen of 14 of the 240 deaths in the current build of slice 1000 (11 on the baseline) and the Policy aims at it. The table does not say how many of those
  Runs held a known flame, frost or wand at the time; that needs the debug records read one by one and was not done here.
- The Goo fight is excluded (`choose` returns null when a Goo is among the enemies) because the sealed room is already handled by `Goo.volley`.
  A Goo variant (`Pack.GOO`) was not built; the numbers above give no case for it.
- Stones of fear, corruption, warding, regrowth and transfusion were left out: fear is temporary, the others do not damage or are weak at full
  health. Wand of fireblast was left out because the fire lands near the hero and a safe check costs more than the gain.
