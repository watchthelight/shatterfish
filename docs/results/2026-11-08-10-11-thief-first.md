# Story 10.11: hit the thief first (no rule kept)

Issue #357. The [measurement page](2026-11-07-10-11-thief-and-bandit.md) found that most Runs which reach depth 5 lose an item to a
thief and that 61 percent of thefts are never recovered. PR #442 added the items-stolen and got-away rows to the direction table.
This page tries the rule the numbers pointed at and records that it does not move the table. `brain/` is unchanged.

## The rule tried

A thief steals on a hit and then runs (`actors/mobs/Thief.java:128-133`, `:146-160`), and teleports away with the item once it is
out of view and 6 or more cells off (`Thief.java:197-223`). With a thief and another enemy both beside the hero, `Fight.attack()`
hit the thief first (a name ending in "thief" or "bandit"). Nothing else changed: no chase, no new retreat. The first test put a
rat and a thief side by side and failed on `main` (the rat was hit), then passed with the rule.

## Numbers

Standard set, `--jobs 1`, baseline pinned to `7ea59b6fa`, current `77d52926a` (the rule on top of that). Theft rows are summed from
the per-Run records, since the ALL-class table does not print them.

| Slice | Runs | Reach depth 5 (base / rule) | Goo kills | Items stolen | Got away |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All classes, salts 1000..1039 | 240 | 135 / 135 | 113 / 112 | 294 / 293 | 170 / 167 |
| All classes, salts 3000..3039 | 240 | 129 / 130 | 102 / 103 | 231 / 227 | 113 / 110 |
| Pooled | 480 | 264 / 265 (+1) | 215 / 215 (0) | 525 / 520 | 283 / 277 |
| Warrior, 80 Runs | 80 | 56 / 56 | 46 / 46 | 131 / 124 | 76 / 66 |

Per class, no class lost more than one Run at depth 5 or one Goo kill on either all-class slice, so the per-class veto of -8 is not
near. The effect on items is a few percent fewer thefts and about 2 percent fewer lost items, inside the spread between two
salt slices of the same Brain (294 against 231 thefts). The one slice with a visible drop (Warrior, 10 fewer got away) did not
change reach or Goo kills either.

## Why it is flat

- The hero already attacks whatever is adjacent in most thief fights, and a thief usually arrives alone; the rule only changes
  the case where a second enemy stands beside it.
- A thief's first hit lands before the hero can answer in most meetings, since it closes on the hero as any mob does, so the
  steal is decided before a target choice exists.
- What does the loss is the flight: 61 percent of stolen items leave with a thief that outruns the hero. A rule that helps would
  be a chase while the thief is in view, which costs turns and exposes the hero to other mobs, and no number here supports it.

## Result

The rule is dropped. The branch is not merged. What stays from the story: the measurement page, the theft rows (PR #442), and
this page. A chase-while-in-view rule is the only untried part; it would need the theft rows printed in ALL-class tables first
(`DirectionHarm.rows` is skipped when a table covers every class).
