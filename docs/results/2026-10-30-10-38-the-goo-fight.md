# Story 10.38: the Goo fight on depth 5

Issue #422. Story 10.7 found Goo behind 126 of the 157 depth-5 deaths (80.3%), 91 of them with the hero hungry or starving
([the diagnosis](2026-10-30-10-7-the-prison-diagnosis.md)). This page says why the fight is lost, what one change did, and what is left.

## Why Goo wins

Every mechanic below was read from the pinned code (Goo.java, Mob.java), not remembered.

1. **The wait for Goo is a free turn for Goo.** Goo walks to the hero a cell a turn, starts a pump-up only from beside the hero, and the pumped
   blow lands two turns later (Goo.java:141-152, :178-226, Mob.java:1383-1392). The fight Policy waited for Goo to arrive while the staff, the
   bow and the missiles were kept out of any fight with Goo in view (`Zap`, `Shoot` and `Ranged` each exclude Goo). Every cell Goo walked was a
   turn the hero spent doing nothing. This is the cause the change below removes. Mage and Huntress, the two classes with a ranged kit, lost the
   most Runs to it: 30 and 27 Goo deaths of 126 in the diagnosis.
2. **Goo heals standing in water.** It regains 1 HP an act on water. In the debug records of the fights it was standing in water on 51% to 58% of
   the fight Decisions. A melee class trades blows for longer and loses the long damage race (Duelist 28, Cleric 9, Warrior 15, Rogue 17 Goo
   deaths). Not changed here; see "What is left".
3. **The seal removes the way out.** The floor seals the turn Goo wakes and refuses every transition (Goo.java:134-136), so resting upstairs has
   to happen before the seal. The hero's HP at the seal averages 0.92 to 0.94 of its maximum on both slices below (Duelist about 0.93 to 0.96),
   so a missing rest is not what loses the fight. Hunger does not tick while sealed.
4. **Hunger decides who arrives weak.** Of the 126 Goo deaths 91 were of a hungry or starving hero (diagnosis). That is the food story, 10.39, and is
   not touched here.
5. **Why `fallback` fires on depth 5 for Huntress and Rogue.** The uniform 1/9 `fallback` Decisions with no enemy in view are not in the Goo fight.
   In the Rogue Run read, the hero was starving and looping through `descend`, rest and search on depth 5 before it woke Goo. They belong to
   the hunger problem and go to 10.39.

## The change

`Goo.volley`: while Goo is two cells off or more and the line is clear, the hero fires at it instead of waiting. The order is the staff bolt,
the bow, then the best plain missile in the pack (`goo: volley zap`, `goo: volley shoot`, `goo: volley throw`). Beside Goo nothing changes, the
blow is the melee one. A pending pump is still dodged first (the dodge is ranked before the volley). `Zap`, `Shoot` and `Ranged` expose the choice
they already made (`zapAt`, `shotAt`, `throwAt`) and still exclude Goo in their own `choose`, so no other fight changes. The volley adds no number,
so the Playbook and the tunables registry are unchanged. Rule row `goo-volley` in `docs/brain-rules.md`; CoachLine sentences for `goo/volley`,
`goo/position` and `goo/wash` (the last two had none).

Test: `GooTest.volley_throws` and `volley_order` fail on `main` (ADR-0019) and pass here; `volley_holds_its_fire` and
`volley_yields_to_the_dodge` pin the cases where nothing may be fired.

## What is left

- **Water.** Goo stands in water for half of the fight and heals. A positioning rule that prefers ground whose predicted arrival cell is dry was
  designed (extend `Goo.position`, fall back to the old condition when none is reachable within `meet_range`) but not built or measured in this
  story. It is the lever for the melee classes, which the volley does not move (Rogue, Cleric and Duelist are unchanged within one Run).
- **A pump that has started cannot be dodged by the volley.** The dodge is ranked first, so a pending pump is still avoided; the fairness review
  found no leak and asked that this stay in the tests (`volley_yields_to_the_dodge`).
- **Food.** 91 of 126 Goo deaths were hungry or starving: story 10.39.

## Kept

The volley, on both slices and on the practice floor.
