# Issue #219, step 6: keep out of a red sentry's floor

**A direction check, unregistered.** Nothing here ran under a Registration and no sequential test was run. It is the
measurement behind the change that makes the Brain remember a red sentry once seen and walk none of the special floor
it guards (`Sentry`, `Explore.walkable`, `Memory.sentries`, [the claim](../brain-rules.md#sentry-floor), [the rule](../rules/levels.md)).
Upstream `v4.0.2`, Profile 6, the `shatterfish` Brain on `weights/shatterfish.json`, Oracle off. Seeds from the
`standard` set only.

Commands (the baseline is `origin/main` at `7b1212eea`, built in a worktree by the tool):

```
./gradlew :rig:direction --args="--set standard --class WARRIOR --first 40 --salt-base 1000 --baseline origin/main --jobs 2 --debug-record"
./gradlew :rig:direction --args="--set standard --class WARRIOR --first 80 --salt-base 3000 --baseline origin/main --jobs 2 --debug-record"
```

## What the sentry deaths were

On the 120 baseline Runs, six died to the red sentry, all on depths 2 to 4 (three on each slice). In each the strategy log shows the
same: the hero walks the room's special floor, for a chest in the chest Policy or for a frontier or search spot in the explore Policy, while
the sentry fires every turn (`SentryRoom.java:253-272`, for `2 + depth / 2` to `4 + depth` a shot, `:292`), and nothing turns it back as its
health goes. The sentry is drawn neutral, so the fight Policy never sees it; it never moves (`:229`) and takes no damage (`:323`), so there is
nothing to fight and nothing to outwait except by leaving its sight. A careful player at that screen does not cross a red sentry's floor for a
chest at thirty hit points: it takes the plain strip along the wall or leaves the chest. The charge before the first shot (`:155`) restarts when
the hero is off the floor (`:279`), which is why a short crossing can be free and a long one is not. The Swarm deaths (also six on the baseline)
were looked at and are several causes at once (fights in the open that split the swarm, stairs ping-pong, being cornered at low health, all
mixed with hunger); they are not touched here.
