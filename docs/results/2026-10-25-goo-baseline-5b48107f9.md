# Goo baseline: 120 Warrior Runs at 5b48107f9

**A direction check, not an acceptance.** Nothing here ran under a Registration and no sequential
test was run. This page gives issue #219 (the Goo gate) a baseline that is less noisy than the 40-Run
tuning set: 120 Runs of `main` with the debug record on, so that every later Goo change is measured
against something with a known spread. It is step 2 of the plan in #219; step 1 was the debug record
itself (PR #223, [ADR-0011](../adr/0011-run-log-format.md)).

Shatterfish `main` at `5b48107f9` (it contains the holiday pin of #228 and the blind-search fix),
upstream `v4.0.2`, the `shatterfish` Brain on `weights/shatterfish.json`, Oracle off, turn cap
20,000. Both batches were played on 2026-10-25 on one machine, one checkout, no baseline side
(`--baseline` is optional, and this page is the baseline).

## Commands

```sh
./gradlew :rig:direction --args="--set standard --class WARRIOR --first 40 --salt-base 1000 --debug-record --jobs 6 --out build/direction/g219-a"
./gradlew :rig:direction --args="--set standard --class WARRIOR --first 80 --salt-base 3000 --debug-record --jobs 6 --out build/direction/g219-b"
```

The first batch is the tuning set (40 Warriors of `standard`, salts 1000 to 1039). The second is
the first 80 Warriors of `standard` under salts 3000 to 3079. Its first 40 seeds are the tuning
seeds again under new salts, and a new salt makes a different Run (see the end of this page), so the
120 Runs are 120 distinct Runs, not a repeat. The numbers below were computed from the Run logs and
their `*.debug.ndjson` records by a scratch script outside the repository (it reads only the
files the two commands wrote). The `goo` and `holdout` sets were not touched.

## What this variance allows

The Goo kill rate is 14.2% with a standard error of 3.2 percentage points at 120 Runs. A
comparison of two independent arms of 120 has a standard error of 4.5 points on the difference, so a
change worth +5 points is a 1.1 sigma effect: not visible.

For +5 points of Goo kills over a 14.2% baseline (14.2% to 19.2%), a two-sided test at 5% with 80%
power needs about **870 Runs per arm** if the arms are independent (90% power: about 1,160). Two
facts soften this a little and neither is measured well:

- Runs that share a seed and salt share a dungeon and a Brain RNG until the change makes them
  diverge, so a paired comparison should correlate. The only correlation visible in this data is
  across salts on the same 40 seeds (below), phi = 0.26 for Goo kills, which would cut the need to
  about 640 Runs per arm. A same-salt pair is probably more correlated than that, but until a real
  pair of arms is played that is a guess.
- The rig's sequential test stops early on a large effect, but a +5 point effect is not large.

So a change whose only claim is +5 points of Goo kills needs on the order of 650 to 900 Runs per
arm, which is 16 to 22 tuning sets, or about 35 to 45 minutes of wall clock per arm at `--jobs 6`
(this 120-Run baseline took 6 minutes, 2 for the first batch and 4 for the second). A change that moves a mechanism the debug record can see (pumps dodged,
potions drunk at the right time) should be judged on that mechanism first, and on Goo kills only at
that sample size.

## Does the salt matter?

Forty seeds were played under both salt bases (1000 and 3000). Goo kills by seed:

| | killed at salt 3000 | not killed at salt 3000 |
|---|---|---|
| killed at salt 1000 | 3 | 5 |
| not killed at salt 1000 | 4 | 28 |

Reaching depth 5 agrees on 24 of the 40 seeds (6 both, 18 neither; 9 only at salt 1000, 7 only at
salt 3000). A seed fixes the dungeon only loosely at these depths, because the salt reaches the Brain's
choices and so everything that follows; the 120 Runs are therefore independent enough for the
intervals above, which treat them as independent Bernoulli trials.

## Reproducing

The Run logs and debug records are in `build/direction/g219-a/current` and `g219-b/current` of the
checkout that played them (about 1.8 MB a record, not committed). Re-running the two commands at
`5b48107f9` on the same upstream tag should reproduce the Runs, since a Run is determined by tag, seed
and action list; this was not re-run to check. The debug record does not change play
(`RunLogRunTest.the_debug_record_changes_nothing`).
